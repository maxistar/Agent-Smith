# Leave and Flexible Work Policy

## Annual leave

Every employee receives 30 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation requests
should be submitted to the manager at least two weeks before the first day away. Up
to five unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used by
the end of March.

## Parental leave

New parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave may start up to two
weeks before the expected birth or placement date and may be taken in one continuous
period or two blocks with manager approval.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on shared office
days, and employees working outside their home country must first contact People
Operations because tax and immigration rules may apply.
