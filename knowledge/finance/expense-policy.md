# Travel and Expense Policy

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any individual expense of 25 EUR or more. The receipt and
business purpose must be attached to the expense report within 30 days of purchase.

## Travel booking

Employees should book standard-class rail and economy-class air travel through the
approved travel portal. A department director must approve international travel
before a non-refundable booking is made.

## Meals

Business meals are reimbursable up to 60 EUR per person, including taxes and tips.
The expense report must list the attendees and explain the business purpose. Alcohol
is not reimbursable unless a director approves it in writing.
